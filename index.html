Download on the App Store Android: coming soon to Google Play

Know when your car will be charged

EV Charge Timer estimates how long charging your electric or plug-in hybrid car takes at home, or which charger amps you need to be done by a set time. It shows the cost, counts down, and rings an alarm when charging should be done. It learns how your own car really charges, so estimates get better over time.

  • Pick your car from a list of popular EVs and plug-in hybrids, or enter the battery size.
  • "Find time" or "Find amps" for your outlet voltage and charger.
  • Charging cost with your electricity price.
  • Live countdown and an alarm, even when the app is closed.
  • Learns from your charges, with an optional guided calibration.
  • Battery Health: an estimate of your battery's capacity and how it changes over the months.
  • English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French and Chinese.
  • No account, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
