Privacy Policy

EV Charge Timer for iPhone and Android · Effective 24 November 2026

Summary

The app has no accounts or analytics, and doesn't collect personal data. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, cars, charge history, calibrations and Battery Health readings (battery levels, odometer readings and their times) are stored only on your phone and in your own phone backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), under your control. The developer can't see them. Deleting the app deletes this data from the phone.

Data the developer receives

None, unless you tap "Share My Charges with the Developer" or "Share with the Developer". These open your phone's share menu, and you choose the app (such as email) and send the message yourself. The shared text contains only charging numbers: car model, battery size, battery levels, charging power, voltage, charging times (for a calibration, the time of each step) and the date. It contains no name, location or device identifiers. Your email address is visible to us only if you send it by email. We use shared charges only to improve estimates, and may add them, without any personal information, to the car table built into the app for other owners of the same car. You can ask us to delete a message you sent at any time.

"Share Report" in Battery Health sends the report only to the person or app you choose; the developer doesn't receive it.

Permissions

The app asks for notification and alarm permissions only to tell you when charging should be done. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Third parties

The app currently contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and sells or shares no data. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for any consent the law requires. Your phone's backup (Apple iCloud or Google) is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
